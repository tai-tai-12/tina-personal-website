I’m a product manager with a technical background in AI/ML, medical imaging, and analytics. I’m most interested in problems where **user needs, technical evidence, and business or operational constraints have to come together in a decision a team can actually execute**.

My path started in medical imaging and AI research, where I learned to evaluate not only whether a model performed well, but why it failed and what the evidence actually supported. As I moved into product management, the questions became broader: **What do users actually need? Which trade-offs matter? What should we ship now, and what should wait?**

Carnegie Mellon University’s M.S. in Business Analytics program extends that foundation through analytics, machine learning, optimization, strategy, and data-driven decision-making.

I’m especially interested in **product roles involving AI/ML and data-intensive technology**, with healthcare as a domain where I bring particularly deep user, workflow, and technical context.
