# V4 Design Notes

## Typography
- Display: Manrope 500/600
- Body/UI: Inter 400/500/600
- Traditional Chinese: Noto Sans TC 400/500/600
- `Tina Tai.` is intentionally medium weight rather than bold.
- `Product & Analytics` has more visual authority than `AI × Healthcare`.
- `AI × Healthcare` is a smaller electric-blue specialization label.

## Color
V4 keeps one true brand color family: blue.

- Near black: `#0B0F19`
- Warm white: `#FCFCFB`
- Cool mist: `#F6F8FB`
- Deep blue: `#1746E0`
- Electric blue: `#2864FF`
- Slate: `#667085`
- Hairline blue-gray: `#DDE4F0`
- Warm ivory atmosphere: `#F7F6F2`

Warm ivory is used sparingly in About and a few quiet surfaces. It is not a second accent color.

## Rationale
The goal is recruiter-first clarity with enough visual authorship to feel personal. V4 avoids the common portfolio pattern of multiple accent colors, overly heavy type, or decorative gradients that compete with the work.
