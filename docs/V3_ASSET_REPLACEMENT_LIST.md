# V3 Asset Replacement List

V3 is intentionally usable before all real visuals are ready. The current project and research visuals are CSS-built placeholders designed to preserve the intended layout.

## Priority 1 — Hero portrait

**Current:** existing professional headshot.

**Later replacement:** newer modern professional portrait.

Recommended source:
- Portrait orientation preferred
- Ideally 4:5 or slightly taller
- Minimum 1600 px on the long edge
- Clean or visually quiet background
- Natural light / professional styling

Target file:
`static/images/profile/tina-headshot.jpg`

Keep the filename the same and the site will update automatically.

## Priority 2 — Flagship case study visual

**Slot:** `From Medical AI Research to a Deployable Product`

Useful source materials:
- Public-safe product / clinical workflow diagram
- Research → validation → product → regulatory flow
- Anonymized or recreated process figure
- Public conference/paper graphic if rights permit

Avoid:
- Patient-identifiable images
- Internal TFDA files
- Proprietary dashboards or UI
- Confidential hospital material

Recommended final image ratio: 4:3 or 3:2, minimum 1600 px wide.
