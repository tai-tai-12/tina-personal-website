# V6 — Product Judgment & Evidence Upgrade

## Release intent
V6 keeps the mature V5.30 visual system and upgrades the portfolio's substance using the September 2026 PM resume and Career Evidence / STAR Story Bank. The goal is to make product judgment visible without turning the site into an interview transcript or a longer résumé.

## Major upgrades
- Updated the public Resume PDF to the newest PM resume.
- Homepage positioning now emphasizes turning technical evidence into product decisions that work for real users.
- Homepage Product project now leads with ~67% faster AI processing and two-center product work.
- AITEWAN experience now shows recurring physician discovery, latency optimization, release sequencing, and TFDA execution.
- Medical AI Product case was substantially rewritten around three actual product decisions:
  1. latency / sensitivity / usability trade-off and the deployed progress indicator;
  2. institution-level operating points based on workflow needs and competitor benchmarks;
  3. sequencing a late model upgrade after estimating ~3–6 months of downstream revalidation/documentation impact.
- Clinical AI case adds a Clinical Insight → Technical Design callout based on the glioma-vs-metastasis error-analysis story.
- Third-Party Testing case now communicates end-to-end external-client ownership, nonconformity and retesting communication, and formal reporting.
- Research outputs now identify MIDL and AMLDS as first-author work.
- Added local portfolio links for public-facing MIDL, AMLDS, and TSBME research artifacts.

## Deliberate exclusions / guardrails
- IRB ambiguity, difficult-colleague, and demanding-stakeholder stories remain private interview material rather than public portfolio content.
- Degree certificates, transcripts, acceptance letters, and reviewer forms were not added to the public site.
- V6 does **not** import the newer resume's 12,437 / 73.2% / 90.13% research-summary metrics into the Clinical AI case. The case continues to use the final-thesis independent-cohort metrics because the supplied sources do not establish that these figures use the same cohort and denominator.
