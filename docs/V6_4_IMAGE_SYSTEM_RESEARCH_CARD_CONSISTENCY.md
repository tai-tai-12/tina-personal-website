# V6.4 — Image System, Research Card Consistency & Cross-Page Visual Alignment

## Release goal

Create one coherent image and preview-card grammar across the portfolio while preserving the distinct roles of Work, Research, About, Education, and recognition content.

## Key changes

- Rebalanced large headings against supporting text instead of simply increasing every type size.
- Unified proof rows on Homepage, Work, and Research into one pill-based evidence component.
- Forced proof rows to stay on one line on large desktops and wrap predictably on smaller screens.
- Rebalanced Homepage Selected Work into a more even text/image split and reduced oversized title behavior.
- Normalized Work and Research preview visuals to a 16:10 editorial frame.
- Rebuilt the Thoracic Aorta research preview from authentic project imagery rather than using a cropped infographic.
- Created a matched Brain Tumor research preview at the same ratio.
- Made Brain Tumor MRI AI and Thoracic Aorta CT Analysis use the same Research card structure: image → meta → title → description → proof → CTA.
- Unified Research CTA wording to `View research →`.
- Kept Thoracic Aorta as foundational research with its own research note rather than promoting it to a fourth Work case.
- Separated Aorta preview, detail hero, and technical evidence roles so the same image is not repeated through the page.
- Tightened Education header-to-content spacing and anchored the credential rail higher on desktop.
- Reduced Footer CTA weight and kept a single primary email.
- Added a site-level image usage map to prevent future duplication and style drift.

## Content guardrails

V6.4 does not resolve or alter disputed / differently-defined dates or brain-tumor cohort metrics. It preserves the existing public definitions and focuses on presentation consistency.
