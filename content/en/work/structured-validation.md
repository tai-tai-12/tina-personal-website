Before my later product and clinical-AI work, I built experience in technical validation and regulatory testing for ionizing-radiation equipment. The work taught me how to make complex evidence traceable, repeatable, and decision-ready.

{{< site-img src="images/projects/structured-validation-banner.svg" alt="Structured validation workflow showing evidence intake, quantitative analysis, report preparation, and execution" >}}

## The challenge

Validation work can become fragmented quickly: multiple products, clients, test protocols, reports, audit requirements, and technical measurements all need to stay consistent.

## My role

As a test manager and research assistant, I conducted third-party testing, prepared regulatory evidence, coordinated audits and seminars, and built analytical tooling to standardize measurement workflows.

## Approach

- Break complex validation requirements into structured test and documentation steps.
- Standardize calculations and measurements through reusable analytical tooling.
- Keep evidence traceable from technical test to final report.
- Coordinate external clients and government-review requirements.

## Results & impact

- Conducted third-party testing for **8 ionizing-radiation equipment products**.
- Delivered **10+ regulatory certification reports**.
- Helped **5 clients** prepare compliance evidence for product review.
- Coordinated **2 government audits** and client seminars.
- Built MATLAB tooling adopted by **2 university labs** to standardize bone-mineral-density quantification.

## What I learned

Structured operations are a form of analytics. Good systems make evidence easier to retrieve, decisions easier to explain, and quality easier to scale.
