I began in medical imaging and AI research, learning to ask not only whether a model performed well, but **where it could fail and what the evidence actually supported**.

Product management changed the questions I ask: What do users need? Which trade-offs matter? What should a team ship now—and what should wait? At AITEWAN, I applied that thinking to clinical AI workflows, physician feedback, and product release decisions.

Now at **Carnegie Mellon University's Tepper School of Business**, I'm strengthening my analytics and business decision-making foundation. I'm particularly interested in AI-enabled and data-intensive products, with healthcare as an area of deeper domain experience.
