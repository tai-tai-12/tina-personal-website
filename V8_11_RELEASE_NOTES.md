# V8.11 — Recruiter Readability Refinement

## Priority change — Experience

The older NYCU BIPI **Test Manager / Research Assistant** section is now notably shorter. The main AITEWAN Product Manager role stays first and carries the most complete product accomplishments and recognition.

The BIPI Experience entry now contains:
1. Short responsibility statement connecting faculty-introduced customer follow-up and hands-on consortium-linked X-ray testing.
2. One compact scope card (`8` products/devices, `5` client organizations, `10+` reports).
3. **Two** contributions: client/consortium coordination and direct testing/reporting.
4. One concise independent MATLAB small-animal CT research note and links to the detailed testing case plus TAF scope.

The complete third-party testing workflow, faculty referral context, consortium membership work and accreditation limitations are preserved in both full case-study languages. No client names or unverified sales outcomes were added.
