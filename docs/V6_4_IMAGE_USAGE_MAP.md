## Rules

- Preview visuals and detail evidence should not use the same image.
- A non-logo image should normally appear in no more than two contexts.
- Research previews use a consistent 16:10 frame, but their content remains project-specific.
- Real conference and award photos remain documentary evidence and are not converted into UI illustrations.
- Technical figures may use a light evidence canvas even when preview artwork is dark.
